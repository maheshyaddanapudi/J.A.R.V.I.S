# Longitude-XL gap ledger (living document)

**Purpose.** One place for every gap the 1000-day run has exposed — in the memory system, in
the agent's behaviour, in the experiment design, and in the instrument — with the evidence
that backs it and the candidate refinement. This is the worklist for the refinement pass
after the run; nothing here is fixed mid-run (instrument-vs-system doctrine, D-0080 records).
Final numbers as of simulated day 1000 (2026-09-11); the second-act record is
`LONGITUDE_XL_ACT2_2026-09-11.md`. Every "(second act)" figure below covers days 501–1000
unless a day range is given.

## Efficiency gaps (cost, tokens, latency, wall-clock)

Measured on the whole life through day 1000 ($172.54 in the world's ledger; first act $70.09, second act $102.45 — attempt 1 of the second act, rolled back, is not in the table). Sources: `model_calls` (tokens per call by role), `metrics.jsonl` (spend and latency per day), `audit_log` (steps per agent run, tool result sizes), `lab_experiments` (tokens per experiment). Cost split below at list prices (Sonnet 5 $3/$15, Haiku 4.5 $1/$5 per M input/output tokens), which reproduces the ledger total within a dollar.

## How to refine from here

1. Strict re-score of every battery from the raw answers (G-13), then re-derive the layer numbers.
2. Rank by attributable misses over days 501–1000: G-01 and G-03 dominate the old-world 8.7 % (63 misses); the twin family G-02/G-04/G-16 accounts for 5 single-hop wrong values, 7 two-hop misses and 2 retirement misses; G-05 is all 7 chapter-two misses; G-07 3 two-hop misses. The cheapest wins are G-05 (two small levers, three captured objectives as tests) and G-16/G-02 (one recall-fallback rule).
3. Each fix gets the D-0080 treatment: spec, unit tests from the exact captured tool arguments, a Sonnet-5 mini-life. E-01 (prompt caching + tool-catalogue trim) is worth doing before the next act — it is 78 % of the bill.
4. Then a **third act on the preserved day-1000 snapshot with chapter three** (late deep-topic corrections G-09, a late user re-pin G-10, unique nicknames G-12; re-arm the relaunch Routine and keepalive, G-14) so the field verification stays on one kernel build. `jarvis_xl` and `/tmp/longitude_xl` are preserved for it — never reset the world.
